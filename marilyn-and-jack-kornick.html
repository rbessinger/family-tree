Schatzman and Levin

Stories from Marilyn and Jack Kornick

Find on the treeAll stories

Sources: family records and the Levin research project, October 2026. Marilyn's funeral and burial are from her Chicago Tribune death notice of December 1, 2008. The trust case passage is from the published federal opinion named in the text.

Sixty-one years

Marilyn Harriet Levin was born May 28, 1927, in Chicago, the younger daughter of Irving Levin and Etta Nathan. She married Jack Kornick, and they stayed married for over 61 years, as her death notice puts it, a span that carried them from the 1940s to 2008. Marilyn died November 30, 2008. Her service was held December 2, 2008, at the Weinstein Funeral Home North Shore Chapel in Wilmette, and she was interred at Memorial Park Cemetery in Skokie, the same cemetery where her sister Ruth rests.

Jack and the carpet business

Jack Kornick was born April 17, 1925, in Chicago, and built his working life in the carpet business, running Jack Kornick & Associates. He was a son of Ben H. Kornick and Fanny Kornick, and the brother of Irving Kornick and of Lorraine, who married Hyman Spreckman. Jack died July 25, 2014, six years after Marilyn. Their sons were Barry Kornick (January 27, 1949 to December 19, 2025) and Randi Kornick.

The trust case, a public record curiosity

The Kornick name survives in one unusual place: a published federal court opinion, Schatzman v. Talley (91 F.R.D. 270), about the Ben H. Kornick Trust, in which Fanny Kornick was the income beneficiary and Jack, Irving, and Lorraine appear as Ben's children. It is a dry document, but it confirms the family's shape from an unexpected direction, and it ties this story to the Schatzmans by name as well as by marriage. It is noted here as a curiosity of the public record, not as family lore.
