Stories from Vivian Schatzman

Find on the treeAll stories

Sources: family records and the Schatzman research project, October 2026. Paul Bessinger's naming is per his wife Wendy, on a recorded call on October 16, 2026. The history session passage is a family lead, marked as sought where it appears.

The youngest, and the longest lived

Vivian Schatzman was born December 1, 1925, in Chicago, the youngest child of Ancil and Sophie Schatzman. She outlived everyone in her generation of the family, dying June 19, 2024, at 98. When her father died in 1978, his obituary listed her as Mrs. Vivian Handel, a daughter among the survivors; she had another forty-six years still to live.

Two marriages, and a namesake

Vivian married first Dr. Paul Schutz; their sons are Ronald and Charles Schutz. She married second Richard Handel. The Schutz marriage left a mark on the wider family that is still in use: her nephew Robert Bessinger named his son Paul after Dr. Paul Schutz (per Wendy Bessinger, recorded call, October 16, 2026). So the Paul who carries this whole project forward in the present generation carries Dr. Schutz's name with him.

The keeper of the history

Late in her life, Vivian sat with her cousin Marcia Katin, who hosted her for what the family calls "a whole history thing," a session where Vivian walked through the family's past. The family believes a VIDEO of that session may exist: Marcia may still have it, Paul may have it, and the family is asking. It is the project's most wanted artifact, marked here as sought, not found. If it surfaces, Vivian's own voice becomes the narrator of half the stories in this set, which is exactly what she seems to have intended.
