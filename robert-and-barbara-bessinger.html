Stories from Robert and Barbara Bessinger

Find on the treeAll stories

Sources: family records and the Bessinger / Schatzman research project, October 2026. The ashtray detail is per Wendy Bessinger, on a recorded call on October 16, 2026. This story is deliberately short: it says what is known and names what is not.

What the record holds

Robert John Bessinger was born April 2, 1943, in Chicago, a son of Ralph and Sybil Bessinger. Barbara Ann Schatzman was born July 22, 1944, a daughter of Marvin Schatzman and Ruth Levin. They married, and their son is Paul Andrew Bessinger. Robert died December 24, 2003. Barbara died May 13, 2020. That is the whole of the documentary record the project has assembled for them so far, and it is thinner than it should be for the couple at the center of this family's living memory.

What is still open

Robert's occupation, whether he served in the military, and where he is buried are open questions, and they are being put directly to Paul, who is being interviewed for this project; whatever he adds will be written here with his name on it. A yahrzeit list at Congregation Beth Shalom of Northbrook names a Robert Bessinger alongside Sybil, which is a lead toward his burial, not a confirmation.

The ashtray

One object carries Barbara's care for this family's history better than any document. In the family home sits an ashtray with the name "ANCIL" written on it, Ancil Schatzman being Barbara's grandfather. Barbara kept it, and it has stayed in the family. His mother's keepsake of her grandfather is still in the house, waiting for its photograph to join the project sources.
