Stories from Marvin Schatzman

Find on the treeAll stories

Sources: family records and the Schatzman / Bessinger research project, October 2026. Marvin's banking role is stated in his father Ancil's 1978 newspaper obituary.

From groceries to the bank chairman's office

Marvin Eugene Schatzman was born March 19, 1915, in Chicago, the oldest child of Ancil and Sophie Schatzman. He followed his father into Mid-City Wholesale Grocers, then made a second career in banking. By the time of his father's death in 1978, Marvin was chairman of Republic Bank of Chicago, which is how Ancil's obituary introduces him to the world: the grocer's son, named first among the survivors, running a bank. He lived a long life, dying January 23, 2009, at 93.

Marriage and family

Marvin married Ruth Levin, born March 30, 1921, a daughter of Irving Levin and Etta Nathan. Their daughter Barbara Ann, born July 22, 1944, married Robert John Bessinger, tying the Schatzman and Bessinger lines together in the generation above Ross. Marvin was, by every account the project has gathered, the senior figure of his generation: when his little brother Walter died in 1924 at age five, Marvin was nine, old enough to remember, and the memory of a sibling lost young survived in the family through him. Ruth outlived him by eight years, dying February 17, 2017; she is buried in Skokie.

Notes for the family

Marvin's own obituary and a fuller account of his years at Republic Bank are still worth pulling; his story here leans on his father's obituary and the family's records. His granddaughter Barbara kept family keepsakes, including an ashtray marked with his father's name, Ancil, that survives in his great-grandson's family today.
