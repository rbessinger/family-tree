Schatzman and Levin

Stories from Irving and Etta Levin

Find on the treeAll stories

Sources: family records and the Levin research project, October 2026. Dates and burials are from death records, burial records, and the Ancestry hint sweep of October 2026. Etta's mother's identity is an open conflict and is marked as such.

Irving

Irving Levin was born March 9, 1897, in Chicago, a son of Max H. Levin and Fannie Bernstein. Max had come from Russia, born December 15, 1877, and died January 7, 1924, in Chicago; he was buried at Waldheim. Fannie, by the consistent set of records found in the 2026 hint sweep, died September 30, 1970. Irving died May 8, 1975, and was buried the next day at Westlawn Cemetery in Norridge.

Etta of O'Brien Street

Etta Nathan was born October 26, 1896, at 75 O'Brien Street, Chicago, an address preserved on her birth record and matched exactly by an independent member tree. Her father was Barnett Nathan, born about 1870 in Russia, who died, by the records found in the sweep, on January 24, 1939. Her mother was Anna Greenberg. The tree long carried her maiden name as Polishuk, but Etta's birth certificate and her parents' January 8, 1893 Cook County marriage record give the name as Annie Greenberg or Greenburg, no record supports Polishuk, and Ross settled it as Greenberg on Oct 19, 2026. Anna, by the sweep's records, was born December 16, 1873, and died in late May 1943. Member trees give Etta siblings Bertha, David, Bessie, Isadore Irving, and Lillian; those are leads, not yet on this tree.

Their family

Irving and Etta married on August 17, 1919, in Chicago. Their daughters were Ruth, born March 30, 1921, who married Marvin Schatzman, and Marilyn, born May 28, 1927, who married Jack Kornick. Etta died September 26, 1978, and was buried at Westlawn beside Irving. Between them they stand at the crossing of two of this family's main lines: their granddaughter Barbara Schatzman married Robert Bessinger, and their grandson-in-law Jack Kornick's family fills its own story in this set.
