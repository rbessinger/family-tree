The story of Jack Siegel

Find on the treeAll stories

Sources: family testimony from a recorded phone call with his wife Marsha Siegel on October 16, 2026, and from recorded calls with his sister Gloria "Gitty" Siegel and his daughter Wendy Bessinger the same day.

Born at 15th Street, married from Talman

Jack Siegel was born June 25, 1945, in Chicago, the son of Samuel Benjamin Siegel and Anne (Greenberg) Siegel. He had no middle name; both his wife Marsha and his daughter Wendy confirmed that, and the question is closed. His sister Gitty remembered the family living at about 3115 West 15th Street when he was born; he grew up in the family home at 6550 North Talman Avenue near Devon and married from that house, as Gitty had before him (Gitty Siegel, October 16, 2026).

Which high school: an open question

The family disagrees about Jack's school, and both versions are kept. Marsha said Jack went to Mather High School (Marsha Siegel, October 16, 2026). Gitty said Jack went to Senn. Marsha's account has a detail in its favor: Mather opened in 1959, when Jack was fourteen, so he could have been in its early classes. The conflict is unresolved and marked that way in the tree.

Cut Rate Toys and Maude Hecht

After high school, Jack went to Miami University in Ohio for two years, then came home and attended Mayfair Junior College in Chicago (Marsha Siegel, October 16, 2026). He worked part time at Cut Rate Toys, a toy store on Devon Avenue, and around the time he and Marsha married, the store's owner, Maude Hecht, invited him to become her partner (Marsha Siegel, October 16, 2026). A teenager called "Marky" worked at the store for a few years and was driven there by Jack; whether Marky was Jack's nephew Monte or Mark Machefsky is still an open family question, and a family friend, Tommy Tremel, worked there too (Gitty Siegel, October 16, 2026).

H&J Liquidators

After Wendy went off to college, Jack moved on from the toy business to H&J Liquidators, a closeout and liquidation business, where he worked for the rest of his career (Marsha Siegel, October 16, 2026).

Marsha, and their daughters

Jack married Marsha, born Fern Marsha Baratz. Their daughters are Wendy Ellyn, who married Paul Bessinger, and Jennifer, who married Richard Katin. He died March 12, 2001, at 55, and was buried at Shalom Memorial Park in Arlington Heights (burial record). His father Sam's 2009 obituary names him as "the late Jack (Marsha) Siegel" (Chicago Tribune, September 22, 2009).
