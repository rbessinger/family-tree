Stories from Ancil and Sophie Schatzman

Find on the treeAll stories

Sources: family records and the Schatzman / Bessinger research project, October 2026. Ancil's career and funeral details are from his 1978 Chicago newspaper obituary clipping, saved in the project sources. Record facts are cited to the records named in the text.

Ancil and Mid-City

Ancil Schatzman was born in Drashna, Russia, a son of Samuel Schatzman. His birth date comes down in three versions: his death record says August 15, 1893, his naturalization record says December 25, 1892, and a Social Security record says December 25, 1890. He came to Chicago as a boy and built his life in the grocery business. His 1978 obituary calls him a founder and former executive of Mid-City Wholesale Grocers, where he worked for more than 50 years before retiring about ten years before his death. He died on August 24, 1978, at Weiss Memorial Hospital, living at the end at the Northwest Home for the Aged on North California Avenue. His services were held at Congregation Rodfei Zedek in Hyde Park, the family congregation.

Sophie of Mogilev

Sophie Edelson was born in Mogilev, in what is now Belarus, in August 1895; her death record gives August 21, her naturalization petition August 23, and Social Security August 24. Her parents were Jacob Edelson of Mogilev and Eva Shapotkin. The family arrived in New York on September 4, 1903, sailing from Bremen, when Sophie was eight. Records also show Edelson siblings for her, Isadore, Elizabeth "Betty" Plavnik, and Emily Chelin, whose stories are still being recovered. Sophie married Ancil in Chicago on June 21, 1914. She died on August 2, 1975, and was buried at Waldheim Cemetery in Forest Park.

Their children

Marvin, the oldest, was born March 19, 1915, and followed his father into groceries before going into banking. Walter, born in March 1919, died on November 3, 1924, at age five, and was buried at Waldheim; for years the family story remembered only that a sibling had died young, and the records gave Walter back his name. Janet, born June 23, 1921, married Milton Rappeport and died April 12, 1972. Vivian, born December 1, 1925, married Dr. Paul Schutz and later Richard Handel, and lived to 98. Ancil's obituary names his survivors as his son Marvin, his daughter Mrs. Vivian Handel, five grandchildren, and two great-grandchildren; Janet had died six years before him.

Notes for the family

Ancil's three birth dates stay in the record as found; no single one is proven. An ashtray with "ANCIL" written on it, kept by his granddaughter Barbara, is still in the family today, a small piece of him.
