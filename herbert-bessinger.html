Stories from Dr. Herbert Bessinger

Find on the treeAll stories

Sources: family records and the Bessinger research project, October 2026. Herbert's career details are from his 2006 Chicago Tribune obituary (Legacy id 2461313). The section on his wife is an open question and is marked as such.

The doctor

Dr. Herbert Edwin Bessinger was born February 9, 1920, in Chicago, a twin with his sister Ruth Janet, and a son of Leopold and Edith Bessinger. He earned his medical degree at the University of Illinois, served in the U.S. Army, and joined the staff of Weiss Memorial Hospital in 1958. He stayed thirty years, until 1988, and built much of the institution he served: he founded its internal medicine residency program and created its first intensive care unit, twenty beds, at the time the largest in Chicago. The hospital's Herbert Bessinger Award of Excellence is named for him. He died May 20, 2006. His Tribune obituary names no wife, no funeral home, and no cemetery, which is part of why the question below exists.

His family

Herbert's children are Donald Bessinger and Lois Bessinger Dee, known from his obituary and the family record. His twin Ruth married Harold Grinker; her children were Janet, Susan, and David. When Ruth died in May 2004, her Tribune notice named her as the sister of Dr. Herbert Bessinger, the two twins still paired in print at the end.

The open question: Herbert's wife

Who Herbert married is the biggest open question on the Bessinger side, and this document states it plainly rather than guessing. The leads, all unproven: a Cook County marriage record dated July 31, 1942, found among his Ancestry hints and not yet pulled as an image; a flagged 1950 census household in Kentucky showing Herbert with a wife and a child; and a Social Security claims record that gives his birth year as 1918 against 1920 everywhere else. The 1942 marriage record is the decisive document, and it is queued. Family memory may beat the records to it: his nephew Paul is being asked directly, and anything he remembers will be added here with his name on it.
