Stories from Peter Baratz

Find on the treeAll stories

Sources: family records (SS Lapland passenger manifest, 1921; death record) and recorded phone calls with his daughter Marsha Siegel on October 16, 2026, and with Wendy Bessinger on October 16, 2026. Record facts are cited to the record; family stories are attributed to their teller.

Perce becomes Peter

Peter Baratz was born June 12, 1912, in Poland, the son of Pincus Barban and Mirla Barban, who became Philip and Mary Baratz in America. His given name was Perce (Peretz) Barban. His father came to the United States first; Peter followed with his mother and his older sister Bluma, who became Bess. Per the SS Lapland passenger manifest, the three sailed from Antwerp, Belgium, and arrived in New York on October 22, 1921. Peter was nine years old. His younger sister Ida was born later, in Chicago, around 1925.

The Poland photograph

A studio portrait taken in Poland before the crossing shows young Peter with his sister Bess and their mother Mary. Wendy Bessinger holds the print; the caption on the mount, in Wendy's hand, reads: "Papa Pete (Peter Baratz) with Sister + mom in Poland." Peter looks about eight to eleven in it, which dates it to roughly 1920 to 1923, right around the family's 1921 departure from Antwerp (per Marsha Siegel, recorded call, October 16, 2026, and Wendy Bessinger). A clean scan of the front and back is still to be made; the print itself is a photograph of the original, with inscriptions across the bottom that have not yet been transcribed.

A working life

Peter became a civil engineer for the State of Illinois (per Marsha Siegel, recorded call, October 16, 2026). He married Leah Beberman, daughter of Joseph and Anna Beberman. Their daughters are Marsha, born Fern Marsha Baratz, and Barby Baratz. He died on October 15, 1980, at 68 (death record; Marsha Siegel, recorded call, October 16, 2026). Per the family knowledge base, he is buried at Shalom Memorial Park.

His sisters

Bess, the sister in the Poland photograph, married a Hyman whose first name the family is still trying to pin down. Family lore, told by Marsha (recorded call, October 16, 2026), says Bess's husband invented the Venetian blind and made real money at it; the couple lived in a big colonial house on Chase Street near Sheridan Road, backing onto the beach, and later divorced. Their son Sonny Hyman was wealthy and, by family lore, friendly with Dean Martin and Jerry Lewis; their daughter Arlene is now Simone Simon. Ida, the Chicago-born sister, married Hyman Wicht.

The brother who was killed: a family story, unproven

Wendy Bessinger tells a family story that Peter had a brother who was killed in Europe, in a story involving soldiers coming to the door (per Wendy Bessinger, recorded call, October 16, 2026). Marsha, Peter's own daughter, had never heard this story when asked on her recorded call of October 16, 2026. No record of such a brother has been found, and no name is known. The family's hope for an answer is Peter's daughter Barby Baratz, who holds much of this branch's memory. This story is recorded here as family story only, not as fact, and it stays off the tree until someone or some record can support it.
