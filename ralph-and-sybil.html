Bessinger and Burger

Stories from Ralph and Sybil Bessinger

Find on the treeAll stories

Sources: family records and the Bessinger research project, October 2026. Marriage details are from the Cook County marriage index. The Burger funeral page passage is a lead, marked as unproven where it appears.

Ralph

Ralph Joseph Bessinger was born in Chicago on July 15, 1915, the oldest child of Leopold and Edith Bessinger. A small record quirk follows him: his birth certificate and the Social Security death index say July 15, while his draft card, his Social Security claims file, and public records say July 16. The birth certificate, written closest to the event, is the one the family record keeps. Ralph died September 5, 1995, in Chicago.

Sybil of St. Louis

Sybil Minnette Burger was born May 12, 1914, in St. Louis, Missouri, a daughter of John S. Burger and Jennie M. Ebeling, who had married in Bellwood, Illinois, in 1909. Her family name is written Burger in the records; the Social Security Administration carried a variant, Boger, which the family record notes and sets aside. She had a brother, Samuel Burger. The Burger side is the thinnest branch of this tree: Sybil's generation is nearly all we have of it so far.

Their family

Ralph and Sybil married on August 12, 1938, in Cook County. Their son Robert John was born April 2, 1943. Their daughter Barbara Ann, called Barbara A. to keep her distinct from her Schatzman cousin of nearly the same name, was born October 20, 1945; she never married and died June 9, 1975, at 29. Sybil lived to December 22, 2000. Ross Bessinger, Robert's grandson, is named after Ralph (per Ross, October 2026), which is why the name Ralph anchors this branch.

The Burger branch: an unproven lead

In April 2026 a Chicago Jewish Funerals page for John Steven Burger (January 3, 1943 to April 21, 2026), a son of Stanley and Ruth Burger, reached Paul Bessinger from a relative who had sought the family out. This is a LEAD, not a fact: if Stanley Burger was another son of John S. Burger and Jennie Ebeling, he would be Sybil's brother, and John Steven Burger her nephew. The timeline fits and no record yet proves or breaks it; John Steven Burger's Cook County birth record, which should name his parents, is the test the project has queued. Until it lands, the Burger branch stays as Sybil left it: St. Louis born, Chicago married, and mostly still to be found.
