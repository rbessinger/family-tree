Siegel and Greenberg

The story of Anne (Greenberg) Siegel

Find on the treeAll stories

Sources: family testimony from a recorded phone call with her daughter Gloria "Gitty" Siegel on October 16, 2026, and Cook County and census records.

Morris and Esther's daughter

Anne Greenberg was born October 19, 1915, in Chicago, the daughter of Morris Greenberg and Esther Moskowitz. Her parents had married in Cook County on October 12, 1912 (marriage record). Anne grew up knowing her father's older children, Minnie and Abe, as her step-sister and step-brother; family testimony held for years that they were children of her father's later remarriage. The records settled it the other way: Minnie and Abe were Morris's children by his first wife, Yetta Unger, so they were Anne's half siblings, and Esther was the second wife. Marsha Siegel confirmed the step-siblings came from the earlier marriage (October 16, 2026), matching the records.

The Tallman house

On November 17, 1935, Anne married Samuel Benjamin Siegel in Cook County. They raised Gloria and Jack (born 1945) in the family home at 6550 North Talman Avenue near Devon, the house both children later married from. It was a crowded, multigenerational house: the 1950 census shows Anne's widowed father Morris heading the household there, with Sam, Anne, Gloria, Jack, and Anne's half brother Abe all under its roof (census record).

Maury's shadow over the house

Anne's brother-in-law Morris "Maury" Siegel, Sam's younger brother, was killed in the Pacific in July 1944, a year before Anne's son Jack was born. The Gold Star mother was Maury's own mother, Miriam Siegel. In the home Anne ran on Talman, Maury's photograph and story stayed present for the next generation; Gitty grew up with her uncle's memory in the house.

Her death

Anne died May 10, 1994, in Chicago and was buried May 12, 1994, at Westlawn Cemetery in Norridge, where Sam would join her in 2009. She was 78. Her grandchildren Monte, Eric, Wendy, and Jennifer are named in Sam's 2009 obituary (Chicago Tribune, September 22, 2009).

One correction the records made

For years, family trees and Ancestry suggestions called Anne's mother "Esther Unger." That was two of Morris's wives merged into one person. Anne's mother was Esther Moskowitz, born 1887 in Piotrkow, Poland, daughter of Vigdor Moskowitz and Gitel (death record). Unger was the maiden name of Morris's first wife, Yetta. The tree now keeps them apart, the way the records do.
